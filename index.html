Why Marginalia

Quick capture

Click the pen, type, save. A few seconds, so it never interrupts your reading.

8 categories

Observations, images, connections, feelings, ideas, lines, drafts, poems.

Reading sessions

Notes auto-attach to the book you're reading, and you can revisit each session later.

Link notes

An observation becomes a line, then a poem. Connect the notes that belong together.

Source text

Attach the exact passage from a PDF or book that inspired the note.

Search

Find anything across notes, titles, books, authors, and tags.

Export

TXT, Markdown, JSON, or PDF. One-click backup and restore.

Any language

English, Nepali, Hindi, Japanese, Arabic — or mix them all freely.

Offline & private

No internet needed. Nothing is ever sent anywhere without your say-so.

Install on your phone

Scan the code with your phone camera. The app page opens and you can install it from there — it then works offline like a native app.

Scan with your phone camera to open the app, then choose Install app / Add to Home Screen.
