What it does

The essentials, nothing more.

Quick capture

Tap the pen, type a line or two, save. Takes a few seconds, so it does not interrupt your reading.

8 categories

Observations, images, connections, feelings, ideas, lines, drafts, and poems.

Reading sessions

Start a session for the book you are reading. Notes taken while it is active attach to it on their own.

Link notes

One note can point to others, so an observation can slowly turn into a line and then a poem.

Source text

Keep the exact passage from the book or PDF that made you stop and write.

Search

Search across note text, titles, books, authors, and tags.

Export

Export as TXT, Markdown, JSON, or PDF. One-click backup and restore.

Any language

Type in English, Nepali, Hindi, Japanese, Arabic, or a mix of all of them.

Offline & private

Works without internet. No analytics, no account, nothing gets sent anywhere.

How it works

Three steps, that is all.

1

Tap the pen

It sits at the edge of your screen while you read, ready whenever you are. Drag it wherever you like.

2

Write it down

Pick a category, type what you noticed, save it. All the fields are optional, so it only takes a moment.

3

Come back to it

Search, link ideas, and review older notes. Small observations can grow into lines, and lines into poems.
